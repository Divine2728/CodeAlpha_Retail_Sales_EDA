# Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project explores retail sales data using **Microsoft Power BI** to identify sales trends, evaluate product category performance, and understand customer purchasing patterns.

The goal was to transform raw retail transaction data into an interactive dashboard that makes it easier to understand business performance and identify opportunities for data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard presents key performance indicators (KPIs), revenue comparisons, customer demographic analysis, and monthly sales trends in a single view.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze overall retail sales performance.
- Track total revenue, quantity sold, and transaction volume.
- Compare revenue generated by different product categories.
- Examine revenue contributions from male and female customers.
- Investigate revenue distribution across customer age groups.
- Identify monthly revenue trends and fluctuations.
- Build an interactive dashboard that allows users to explore the data using slicers.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Data visualization, dashboard development, and interactive analysis.
- **Power Query** — Data preparation and transformation where required.
- **DAX** — Measures and calculations for key performance indicators, where applicable.
- **Retail Sales Dataset** — Transaction-level data containing customer, product, quantity, pricing, date, and sales information.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Age Group Contribution

A column chart compares revenue across customer age groups:

- Under 20
- 20–29
- 30–39
- 40–49
- 50–59
- 60 and Above

The chart helps identify the age groups contributing most to total revenue and those with comparatively lower contributions.

### 2. Gender Contribution

A doughnut chart compares revenue generated by female and male customers.

| Gender | Total Revenue | Revenue Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Female | $232,840 | 51.1% |
| Male | $223,160 | 48.9% |

Female customers generated slightly more revenue than male customers, although the difference was relatively small.

### 3. Revenue by Product Category

The dashboard compares revenue across the three product categories.

| Product Category | Total Revenue | Approx. Revenue Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Electronics | $156,905 | 34.4% |
| Clothing | $155,580 | 34.1% |
| Beauty | $143,515 | 31.5% |
| **Total** | **$456,000** | **100%** |

Electronics generated the highest revenue, followed closely by Clothing. Beauty recorded the lowest revenue among the three categories.

The relatively close results suggest that revenue was distributed fairly evenly across the product categories.

### 4. Monthly Revenue Trend

A line chart visualizes revenue performance from January through December.

The chart helps identify high-performing months, periods of declining revenue, and potential seasonal patterns.

Key observations from the dashboard include:

- **May:** The highest monthly revenue, at approximately $53,000.
- **September:** The lowest monthly revenue, at approximately $23,000.
- **October:** Revenue recovered to approximately $46,000 after the September decline.
- **December:** Revenue increased again to approximately $44,000.

These fluctuations highlight the importance of monitoring monthly sales performance when planning inventory, promotions, and marketing activities.

*Monthly figures are approximate estimates based on the dashboard visualization.*

### 5. Interactive Slicers

The dashboard includes four slicers that allow users to explore different segments of the data:

- **Quarter:** Filter the analysis by Qtr 1, Qtr 2, Qtr 3, or Qtr 4.
- **Gender:** Compare female and male customer segments.
- **Product Category:** Focus on Beauty, Clothing, or Electronics.
- **Age Group:** Analyze sales contributions across different customer age groups.

These interactive controls allow users to investigate specific segments without needing separate dashboards for each analysis.

## Key Findings

The analysis produced several useful observations:

1. **Electronics led product category revenue.** It generated $156,905, slightly exceeding Clothing at $155,580.

2. **Female customers contributed slightly more revenue.** Female customers generated $232,840, compared with $223,160 from male customers.

3. **Revenue varied throughout the year.** May was the strongest month in the displayed trend, while September recorded the lowest revenue.

4. **Revenue differed across age groups.** Customers aged 50–59 formed the leading age group by revenue in the displayed chart, while customers under 20 contributed considerably less.

5. **Product category revenue was relatively balanced.** No single category dominated overall revenue, suggesting that all three categories contributed meaningfully to sales.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard findings, the following actions could be considered:

- **Strengthen top-performing categories:** Investigate which Electronics products drive revenue and maintain appropriate inventory levels.
- **Review the September decline:** Examine transaction-level records to understand whether the decline was associated with fewer transactions, lower spending, or other factors.
- **Investigate customer segments:** Explore product preferences and purchasing frequency across age groups and genders before designing targeted campaigns.
- **Maintain a balanced category strategy:** Continue monitoring all three categories instead of focusing exclusively on the highest-revenue category.
- **Track monthly performance:** Compare revenue across months and quarters to support better sales forecasting and promotional planning.

These recommendations are starting points for further investigation rather than proof of specific causes behind the observed trends.
